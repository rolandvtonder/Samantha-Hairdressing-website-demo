Questions,answered

How do I book an appointment?

+

The quickest way is WhatsApp on 083 254 2414. You can also call, or use the booking form on the contact page — it sends your request straight to Samantha on WhatsApp.

Where is the salon?

+

We are at 211 Monument Road, Glen Marais, Kempton Park, 1619, in Kempton Park on Johannesburg’s East Rand.

How much does a cut or colour cost?

+

Prices depend on your hair length, thickness and the result you want, so we quote after a quick chat. Send a photo of your hair on WhatsApp for a fast estimate.

Yes — gel and acrylic overlays for hands and toes, and you can combine nails with a blow wave in our pamper special.

Ja! Samantha is happy to chat in English or Afrikaans.
